# Product Goals

## Primary Goal

To simplify the daily school experience for students by centralizing essential information like schedules, announcements, and lunch menus into a single, easy-to-access mobile application.

## User-Facing Goals

- Provide students with immediate access to their personalized and school-wide bell schedules.
- Deliver timely and approved school announcements.
- Display the current cafeteria lunch menu.
- Enable discovery of club and activity events.
- Consolidate key daily information, reducing the need to check multiple sources.

## Launch Goals (Pilot Release at Northview High School)

- Successfully deploy and pilot the Campus Companion app at Northview High School.
- Integrate personalized student schedules from the Student Information System (SIS) and provide a school-wide fallback.
- Display approved school announcements sourced from the front-office Google Sheet.
- Show the daily lunch menu from the cafeteria vendor feed, with a graceful "Menu not available yet" message if data is missing.
- Include club and activity calendar items.
- Authenticate students using district Google accounts.
- Limit push notifications strictly to urgent announcements, schedule changes, and emergency alerts.

## Non-Goals For The First Version

- Direct student-to-teacher messaging.
- Student comment threads on announcements.
- Display or storage of sensitive student data such as grades, attendance, discipline records, medical notes, or counseling information.
- Bus route reminders (pending confirmation from transportation).
- Sports game scores.
- Homework assignments display.
- General notifications for all club meetings or lunch menu updates.
